# Design: Manali Jain portfolio

Short summaries on white 3D sheets over a soft green-grey page, with teal, blue and coral accents. Each section is a sheet that tilts in from depth when scrolled into view and leans toward the pointer. The hero has a 3D point-cloud terrain with a small wireframe car (Three.js). No stars, no glow. Reduced motion: no tilt, static terrain.

Motion: smooth scrolling (Lenis), a 3D Taravi mark in the header, a LiDAR scan line that draws the terrain on load, and a timeline line that draws down the page as you scroll. Reduced motion turns these off.
